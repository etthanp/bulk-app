Your Bulk Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Short version: Your Bulk keeps everything on your phone. We don’t collect, see, sell or share your data.

What the app stores

To build your plan, Your Bulk asks for your name (optional), age, sex, height, weight, goal weight and date, activity level, lift days and foods you don’t eat. As you use it, it saves your weigh-ins, the meals, shakes and lifts you check off, and your messages to the Coach.

All of this is stored only on your device (and in your own iPhone backups, if you use them). It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The Coach

The Coach runs entirely on your phone using a built-in list of foods. Your messages are not sent to any server or AI service.

Reminders

Lift reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS. No server is involved.

Subscriptions

Purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy covers that data.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Your Bulk has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn’t use any third-party services that collect data.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings › Erase everything in the app, or delete the app. Either one removes all of your data from the device.

Children

Your Bulk is meant for ages 13 and up. Because we collect no data, we don’t knowingly collect data from anyone. Users under 18 should check with a parent before using supplements like mass gainers.

Health information

Your Bulk gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing your diet if you have a medical condition.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above. Questions: ethan@trulyautomation.com
