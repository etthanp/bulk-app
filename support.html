Need help? Email ethan@trulyautomation.com and we’ll get back to you within 2 business days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app: Settings › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Your Bulk. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends and you won’t be charged.

I got a new phone. Where’s my data?

Your Bulk saves everything on your phone, and it’s included in your iPhone backup. Restore your new phone from that backup and your plan comes back. Use Restore Purchases on the subscription screen to get your subscription back.

I’m not gaining weight

Weigh in most mornings. After 2 weeks, the weekly check-in on the Today screen tells you if you’re behind and adds calories. The fastest fix is usually one more mass gainer shake a day.

Reminders aren’t showing up

Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Your Bulk and make sure notifications are allowed, then open Your Bulk once so it can schedule the next few weeks.
